About

Read more

I am a Scientist C in the Bridge Engineering and Structures Division at CSIR-Central Road Research Institute (Ministry of Science and Technology, Govt. of India), New Delhi, and an Assistant Professor at the Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR). My work connects bridge engineering with computational mechanics, structural health monitoring, and artificial intelligence.

I completed my Ph.D. in Structural Engineering at IIT Roorkee in 2025, following an M.Tech. in Structural Engineering at NIT Hamirpur in 2020. My research includes robust topology optimization, auxetic metamaterials, uncertainty quantification, vibration control, and impact mechanics.
