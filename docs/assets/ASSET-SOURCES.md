# Image and figure sources

## Original role-card sketches

Four graphite/ink-style illustrations were generated specifically for this portfolio with built-in image generation. They are artistic role illustrations, not photographs of a real laboratory, bridge, or the website owner.

- `sketches/scientist.png`: structural laboratory and beam-testing frame.
- `sketches/educator.png`: teaching desk and beam sketch.
- `sketches/computational-researcher.png`: laptop and finite-element mesh.
- `sketches/bridge-engineer.png`: multi-span bridge perspective.

Exact prompts, generation mode, and repository paths are in `content/illustration-prompts.json` at the repository root. The original generated PNGs are included without destructive edits. Their display treatment adapts to the site's theme.

## Original engineering diagrams

The five SVGs in `diagrams/` were authored for the new educational articles. They show conceptual relationships, not construction details, project-specific designs, measured defects, real monitoring data, or acceptance thresholds. Each has an accessible title/description and a contextual article caption. Engineering background sources are linked within the articles; no third-party technical figure has been copied.
