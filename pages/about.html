About

Researcher, designer, and builder working across computational mechanics, software, and entrepreneurship.

I am Abhi Gupta. My work spans computational solid mechanics (phase-field fracture, topology optimization, multi-physics finite-element modeling), software engineering for scientific and educational tools, and the visual communication of technical ideas.

What I do

  • Research — variational models of fracture, design optimization, and reduced-order modeling.
  • Software — open-source codes and pedagogical frameworks built on FEniCS, Python, and JavaScript.
  • Teaching & community — running an online community for structural and computational engineering with hundreds of thousands of learners.
  • Design — scientific illustration, motion graphics, and visual storytelling for research dissemination.

Currently

Building research, code, and writing that brings rigorous engineering mechanics closer to the people who actually use it.
